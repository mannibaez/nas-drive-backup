Privacy policy

This policy describes NAS Drive Backup, a personal utility operated by Manuel Báez.

Data accessed and purpose

With the operator's authorization, rclone accesses Google Drive file contents and metadata, such as filenames, folder paths, sizes, and modification times, to create and update selected backups on the operator's NAS. Google-native documents may be exported to downloadable formats where supported.

Authorization and storage

The utility requests read-only Google Drive access. OAuth tokens and configuration are stored on the operator's NAS to permit scheduled downloads. Backup data and operational logs, which may include filenames and errors, are also stored there. Google passwords are entered only into Google's authentication flow.

Use and disclosure

Google user data is used solely to provide the operator's personal backup. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Backup data and credentials are not published on this website or in its GitHub repository. The backup communicates with Google to retrieve authorized data.

NAS Drive Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Downloaded copies remain on the NAS until the operator deletes them. Removing an original from Google Drive does not automatically remove a retained backup. Updates may replace an existing local copy; any separately configured snapshots or versioned backups may retain earlier versions until their retention periods expire.

The operator may stop the scheduled task and revoke the connection through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not erase existing NAS copies. Delete those copies, logs, and any retained versions separately to remove them.

This website

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process request information under GitHub's privacy statement.
